# Methodology
Loaded the hotel booking dataset using pandas  
Inspected dataset structure and handled missing values in key columns  
Created a proper datetime column for accurate time-based analysis  
Performed aggregations using groupby() and pivot tables across months, hotel types, and customer segments  
Analyzed cancellation behavior using lead time, country, and market segment features  
Built visualizations to explore seasonal demand patterns and booking behavior  

# Business Insights

## Strong Seasonality in Demand
Hotel bookings clearly fluctuate across different months, with demand increasing during peak travel seasons.
→ Hotels should adjust pricing strategies and allocate more resources during high-demand periods to maximize revenue.

## Longer Planning Leads to Higher Cancellation Risk
Bookings made far in advance tend to have a higher chance of being cancelled compared to last-minute reservations.
→ Implementing stricter cancellation policies or offering incentives for non-refundable bookings can help reduce uncertainty.

## Customer Segments Behave Differently
Some market segments show more stable booking behavior, while others are more likely to cancel reservations.
→ Hotels should focus on reliable customer segments and tailor marketing strategies to attract more consistent guests.

## Demand Varies by Hotel Type
Different hotel types experience varying levels of demand across seasons, indicating different customer preferences.
→ Revenue management strategies should be customized for each hotel type instead of applying a single uniform approach.

## Geographic Differences in Booking Behavior
Customers from different countries show varying booking and cancellation patterns.
→ Hotels can use targeted promotions and region-specific strategies to attract high-value and more reliable customers.
